Wi-Fi guidelines

This connection is intended for school learning, teaching, communication, and administrative work.

  • Use your own school network account and keep your password private.
  • Respect other users and avoid activity that disrupts the shared connection.
  • Follow school rules and applicable laws when using online resources.
  • Report access problems or suspicious activity to the school IT coordinator.

These are draft on-screen guidelines. The school should review and approve the final policy before deployment.
